Condition: Symptomatic Bradycardia / Heart Block
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: urgent
Prevalence: uncommon
ICD-10 Code: R00.1
SNOMED Code: 48867003
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • slow heartbeat
  • lightheadedness
  • fatigue
  • syncope
  • dizziness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual or sudden depending on cause. LOCATION: Generalized symptoms. DURATION: Continuous while bradycardic. CHARACTER: Lightheadedness, fatigue, weakness, syncope. AGGRAVATING: Exertion (symptoms worsen with fixed low heart rate). RELIEVING: Increased heart rate (pacing, medications). TIMING: Can be constant or episodic. SEVERITY: Variable, from asymptomatic to syncope. KEY POSITIVES: Heart rate <60 bpm with symptoms, syncope or near-syncope, fatigue, dyspnea on exertion, elderly patient, medications (beta-blockers, calcium blockers), prolonged PR or AV block on electrocardiogram (ECG). KEY NEGATIVES: NOT tachycardia, NOT palpitations.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, sudden, depending on cause
  Negative indicators: chronic, constant, unrelenting

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized symptoms
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, chest

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: continuous while bradycardic, persistent
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: lightheadedness, fatigue, weakness, syncope
  Negative indicators: tachycardia, palpitations, chest pain

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, symptoms worsen with fixed low heart rate
  Negative indicators: increased heart rate, pacing, medications

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: increased heart rate, pacing, medications
  Negative indicators: exertion, activity, low heart rate

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: constant, episodic
  Negative indicators: intermittent, comes and goes, unpredictable

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: variable, asymptomatic to syncope, variable symptoms
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, constant

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Syncope or near-syncope

  🚨 Heart rate <40 bpm

  🚨 Complete heart block (third-degree AV block)

  🚨 Chest pain

  🚨 Dyspnea

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Hypotension

  🚨 Recent myocardial infarction (MI)
