DIAGNOSTIC GUIDELINE: ECTOPIC PREGNANCY
================================================================================

Condition: Ectopic Pregnancy
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: emergent
Prevalence: rare
ICD-10 Code: O00.90
SNOMED Code: 34801009
Sex: female

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • abdominal pain
  • pelvic pain
  • lower abdominal pain
  • vaginal bleeding

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset 6-8 weeks after last menstrual period. Initially mild, becomes SUDDEN SEVERE if rupture. LOCATION: UNILATERAL LOWER ABDOMINAL/PELVIC PAIN. Adnexal region (one side). Shoulder pain if ruptured (hemoperitoneum). DURATION: Initially intermittent cramping over days. Becomes CONSTANT if rupture occurs. Pain progresses over hours to days. CHARACTER: Initially CRAMPY, becomes SHARP and SEVERE if rupture. Stabbing pain with rupture. AGGRAVATING: MOVEMENT may worsen. Physical activity can precipitate rupture. Jarring intensifies if ruptured. RELIEVING: NOTHING relieves effectively. Position doesn't help significantly. Requires surgical intervention (salpingectomy/salpingostomy). TIMING: Initially INTERMITTENT cramping. Becomes CONSTANT and severe with rupture. Progressive worsening. SEVERITY: 4-7/10 initially, 9-10/10 if ruptured. Catastrophic with rupture. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: MISSED PERIOD, vaginal spotting (lighter than period), dizziness, lightheadedness. If ruptured: syncope, peritoneal signs. PREGNANCY TEST POSITIVE. KEY POSITIVES: Positive pregnancy test + unilateral pelvic pain + vaginal bleeding. Prior ectopic, pelvic inflammatory disease (PID), IUD use. KEY NEGATIVES: NEGATIVE pregnancy test essentially rules out.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, acute, immediate, hours, days, weeks
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: pelvic, lower abdominal, unilateral, adnexal, vaginal

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: constant, intermittent
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, constant, continuous

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe, sharp, stabbing, crampy, cramping

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: movement, physical activity, jarring movements

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: nothing relieves, surgical intervention
  Negative indicators: position changes, medications

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: constant, progressively worsening

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 9-10/10, severe

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 LIFE-THREATENING if ruptured - sudden severe pain with hypotension/syncope - call 911

  🚨 Shoulder pain with abdominal pain in pregnant woman - ruptured ectopic with hemoperitoneum

  🚨 Positive pregnancy test + abdominal pain - ALWAYS get ultrasound to rule out ectopic

  🚨 ANY female of childbearing age with abdominal pain needs pregnancy test
