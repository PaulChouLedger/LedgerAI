CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • trigeminal neuralgia
  • facial pain
  • nerve pain
  • facial neuralgia
  • trigeminal nerve pain
  • facial shooting pain
  • facial electric pain

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within seconds. LOCATION: UNILATERAL facial pain. DURATION: Lasts seconds to minutes. CHARACTER: SEVERE, SHARP, ELECTRIC-like pain. AGGRAVATING: TOUCH, speaking, eating, brushing teeth. RELIEVING: ANTICONVULSANTS, avoiding triggers. TIMING: Sudden onset, lasts seconds to minutes. SEVERITY: 8-10/10, severe and unrelenting. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: SEVERE FACIAL PAIN, may have facial twitching, may have difficulty speaking, eating. KEY POSITIVES: Severe facial pain, electric-like pain, trigger points. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, within seconds, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset, progressive

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: unilateral facial pain, one side, facial, unilateral
  Negative indicators: bilateral, both sides, generalized, diffuse

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: seconds to minutes, brief, transient, short
  Negative indicators: hours, days, persistent, ongoing

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe, sharp, electric-like pain, severe and unrelenting
  Negative indicators: mild, dull, aching, moderate

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: touch, speaking, eating, brushing teeth
  Negative indicators: anticonvulsants, avoiding triggers, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: anticonvulsants, avoiding triggers, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: touch, speaking, eating, brushing teeth

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, episodic, intermittent
  Negative indicators: continuous, persistent, unrelenting

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe and unrelenting, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe facial pain with other neurological deficits - possible stroke, call 911

  🚨 Facial pain with signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Facial pain with severe hypertension - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Facial pain with signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Facial pain with signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911
