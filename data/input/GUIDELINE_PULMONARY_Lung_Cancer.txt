CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • lung cancer
  • chest pain
  • cough with blood
  • weight loss
  • shortness of breath

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual over months. LOCATION: Focal or diffuse lung areas. DURATION: Chronic, progressive. CHARACTER: Chronic cough, chest pain, dyspnea. AGGRAVATING: Exertion, lying flat. RELIEVING: Pain medications, oxygen. TIMING: Chronic, progressive. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (5-8/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Chronic cough, hemoptysis, chest pain, dyspnea, weight loss, fatigue, hoarseness, bone pain. KEY POSITIVES: Smoking history, chronic cough, hemoptysis, weight loss. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever (unless infection), no wheezing (unless obstruction).

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual over months, progressive, slow onset
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: focal or diffuse lung areas, lung, chest
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, specific point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic, progressive, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: chronic cough, chest pain, dyspnea
  Negative indicators: no fever, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, lying flat, activity
  Negative indicators: pain medications, oxygen, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: pain medications, oxygen, rest
  Negative indicators: exertion, lying flat, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic, progressive, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 5-8/10, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Massive hemoptysis

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Signs of superior vena cava syndrome

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Severe chest pain

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Signs of metastatic disease
