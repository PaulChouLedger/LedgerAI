--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • severe headache
  • chest pain
  • vision changes
  • shortness of breath
  • high blood pressure

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Acute, over hours. LOCATION: Headache (occipital or diffuse), chest (if cardiac involvement), generalized symptoms. DURATION: Continuous. CHARACTER: Severe headache, dyspnea, chest pain, vision changes, confusion. AGGRAVATING: Medication non-compliance, stress. RELIEVING: Blood pressure reduction. TIMING: Progressive. SEVERITY: 8-10/10 for headache or symptoms. KEY POSITIVES: Severe hypertension (blood pressure (BP) >180/120), end-organ damage (altered mental status, chest pain, dyspnea, vision changes, seizures), headache, nausea, vomiting. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT isolated elevated blood pressure (BP) without symptoms (that's hypertensive urgency).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: acute, over hours, sudden
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: headache, occipital, diffuse, chest, generalized symptoms
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, extremities

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: continuous, persistent, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe headache, dyspnea, chest pain, vision changes, confusion
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, no symptoms

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: medication non-compliance, stress
  Negative indicators: blood pressure reduction, medication, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: blood pressure reduction, medication
  Negative indicators: medication non-compliance, stress, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive, continuous, worsening
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 8-10/10, severe, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, 1-7/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe blood pressure elevation (>180/120)

  🚨 Altered mental status or confusion

  🚨 Severe headache

  🚨 Vision changes or blurry vision

  🚨 Chest pain

  🚨 Shortness of breath

  🚨 Seizures

  🚨 Focal neurologic deficits

  🚨 Nausea and vomiting
