CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • flu
  • influenza
  • fever and cough
  • body aches and fever
  • viral illness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden onset over hours. LOCATION: Systemic, respiratory. DURATION: 3-7 days, can last longer. CHARACTER: High fever, severe body aches, dry cough. AGGRAVATING: Exertion, cold air. RELIEVING: Rest, fluids, antipyretics. TIMING: Acute, seasonal. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (6-8/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: High fever, chills, severe body aches, headache, dry cough, sore throat, fatigue, weakness. KEY POSITIVES: High fever, body aches, dry cough, seasonal. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain (unless secondary), no wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset over hours, acute, immediate
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: systemic, respiratory
  Negative indicators: localized, one side only, specific point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: 3-7 days, can last longer, variable
  Negative indicators: chronic, persistent, ongoing

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: high fever, severe body aches, dry cough
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, cold air, activity
  Negative indicators: rest, fluids, antipyretics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: rest, fluids, antipyretics
  Negative indicators: exertion, cold air, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, seasonal, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 6-8/10, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 High fever >103°F

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Signs of dehydration

  🚨 Chest pain with breathing

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Signs of secondary bacterial infection

  🚨 Severe weakness or inability to move
