CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • spinal cord injury
  • paralysis
  • numbness
  • weakness
  • back injury
  • neck injury
  • trauma

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset after trauma. LOCATION: BELOW level of injury. DURATION: Immediate onset, symptoms persist. CHARACTER: PARALYSIS, numbness, weakness. AGGRAVATING: MOVEMENT, pressure. RELIEVING: IMMEDIATE medical intervention, supportive care. TIMING: Immediate onset, symptoms persist. SEVERITY: 8-10/10, severe and unrelenting. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: PARALYSIS, numbness, weakness, may have bowel/bladder dysfunction, may have respiratory problems. KEY POSITIVES: Paralysis, numbness, weakness, bowel/bladder dysfunction. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, immediate, after trauma, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, progressive, slow onset, days to weeks

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: below level of injury, distal to injury, lower body, affected area
  Negative indicators: above injury, proximal, upper body, unaffected area

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: immediate, persistent, ongoing, permanent
  Negative indicators: brief, minutes, hours, transient

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: paralysis, numbness, weakness, loss of function
  Negative indicators: pain, burning, tingling, sharp

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: movement, pressure, activity, manipulation
  Negative indicators: rest, immobilization, support, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: immediate medical intervention, supportive care, treatment, immobilization
  Negative indicators: movement, pressure, activity, manipulation

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: immediate, persistent, ongoing, constant
  Negative indicators: intermittent, variable, episodic, transient

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10, unrelenting
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, moderate, 4-7/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe respiratory distress - possible respiratory failure, call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911
