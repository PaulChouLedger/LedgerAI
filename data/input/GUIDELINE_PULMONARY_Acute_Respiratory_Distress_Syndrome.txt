================================================================================

Condition: Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: emergent
Prevalence: uncommon
ICD-10 Code: J80
SNOMED Code: 67782005
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • severe shortness of breath
  • respiratory failure
  • can't breathe
  • acute lung injury
  • ARDS

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Rapid onset within hours to days of inciting event. LOCATION: Bilateral lung fields, diffuse. DURATION: Persistent, requires mechanical ventilation. CHARACTER: Severe dyspnea, air hunger, 'drowning sensation'. AGGRAVATING: Any exertion, lying flat. RELIEVING: High-flow oxygen, mechanical ventilation, prone positioning. TIMING: Continuous, progressive. SEVERITY: Severe (8-10/10), life-threatening. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Tachypnea, tachycardia, cyanosis, altered mental status, multi-organ failure. KEY POSITIVES: Severe dyspnea, bilateral infiltrates on chest X-ray (CXR), refractory hypoxemia. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain, no wheezing (unless underlying asthma).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: rapid onset within hours to days of inciting event, acute, immediate
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral lung fields, diffuse
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: persistent, requires mechanical ventilation, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe dyspnea, air hunger, drowning sensation
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: any exertion, lying flat, activity
  Negative indicators: high-flow oxygen, mechanical ventilation, prone positioning

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: high-flow oxygen, mechanical ventilation, prone positioning
  Negative indicators: any exertion, lying flat, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: continuous, progressive, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe, 8-10/10, life-threatening, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Tachypnea >30 breaths/min

  🚨 Use of accessory muscles

  🚨 Inability to speak

  🚨 Hypotension or shock

  🚨 Multi-organ failure
