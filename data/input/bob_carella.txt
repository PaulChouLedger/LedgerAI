BOB CARELLA — CFO, LEDGER AI
======================================

ROLE & BACKGROUND
Bob Carella is the Chief Financial Officer (CFO) of LedgerAI. He manages the company's financial operations, treasury, tokenomics modeling, and financial risk analysis. He works directly with Paul Chou (CEO), David Lara (COO), and Jorge Guinovart (Marketing).

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES
- Treasury management including $LEDGER token holdings
- Tokenomics model design and stress-testing
- Financial runway and burn rate analysis
- Market maker relationships and liquidity planning
- Forensic audit coordination for token-related financial exposure
- Strategic alignment with co-founders on company direction

TEAM DYNAMICS
Bob Carella is one of the co-founders of LedgerAI. He works closely with:
- Paul Chou (CEO & Founder) — product decisions and engineering, overall strategic direction
- David Lara (COO) — enterprise partnerships, legal strategy
- Jorge Guinovart — community engagement and marketing

NOTABLE CONTEXT
Bob Carella has been involved in navigating financial crises related to the $LEDGER token, including treasury exposure analysis, token recovery planning, and coordinating with legal counsel. He has had to manage relationships with market makers and address internal team tensions during periods of market stress.
