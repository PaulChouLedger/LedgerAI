CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • severe chest pain
  • severe back pain
  • tearing pain
  • sudden chest and back pain

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN, maximal at onset. LOCATION: Chest and/or BACK (interscapular). RADIATES along aortic path (chest→back→abdomen as dissection extends). DURATION: Continuous, persistent. CHARACTER: TEARING, RIPPING, or severe sharp pain. AGGRAVATING: Hypertension. RELIEVING: Nothing helps. TIMING: Sudden onset, unrelenting. SEVERITY: 10/10, catastrophic. KEY POSITIVES: Hypertension, pulse or blood pressure (BP) differential between extremities, Marfan syndrome, bicuspid aortic valve, aortic regurgitation murmur. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT gradual onset, NOT pressure (like myocardial infarction ).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, maximal at onset, catastrophic
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, back, interscapular, radiates along aortic path, chest to back to abdomen
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, no radiation

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: continuous, persistent, unrelenting
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: tearing, ripping, severe sharp, catastrophic
  Negative indicators: pressure, dull, crampy, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: hypertension, high blood pressure
  Negative indicators: rest, medication, position

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: nothing helps, no relief
  Negative indicators: medication, position, rest

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, unrelenting, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 10/10, catastrophic, 10, maximal
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, 1-9/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Sudden maximal pain

  🚨 Tearing or ripping quality

  🚨 Back pain (interscapular)

  🚨 Pulse differential

  🚨 Blood pressure differential >20 mmHg

  🚨 Neurologic deficits

  🚨 Marfan syndrome

  🚨 Hypotension or shock

  🚨 Syncope
