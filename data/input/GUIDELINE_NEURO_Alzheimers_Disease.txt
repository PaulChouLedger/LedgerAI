CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • alzheimers
  • dementia
  • memory problems
  • confusion
  • cognitive decline
  • behavioral changes
  • wandering

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over months to years. LOCATION: GENERALIZED cognitive decline. DURATION: Chronic progressive condition. CHARACTER: MEMORY LOSS, confusion, cognitive decline. AGGRAVATING: STRESS, fatigue, medication changes. RELIEVING: CHOLINESTERASE inhibitors, supportive care. TIMING: Chronic progressive, symptoms worsen over time. SEVERITY: 3-7/10, mild to moderate. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: MEMORY LOSS, confusion, cognitive decline, behavioral changes, may have wandering. KEY POSITIVES: Memory loss, confusion, cognitive decline, behavioral changes. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, months to years, slow onset, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate, hours

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized cognitive decline, diffuse, whole brain, bilateral
  Negative indicators: localized, focal, specific area, unilateral

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic progressive, ongoing, persistent, worsening
  Negative indicators: acute, brief, transient, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: memory loss, confusion, cognitive decline, mild to moderate
  Negative indicators: severe, focal deficits, localized, acute

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: stress, fatigue, medication changes, triggers
  Negative indicators: cholinesterase inhibitors, supportive care, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: cholinesterase inhibitors, supportive care, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: stress, fatigue, medication changes, triggers

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic progressive, worsening over time, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: mild to moderate, 3-7/10, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7
  Negative indicators: severe, 8-10/10, mild, 1-2/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible severe Alzheimer's flare, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911
