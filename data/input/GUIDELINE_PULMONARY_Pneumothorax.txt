CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • pneumothorax
  • collapsed lung
  • chest pain
  • shortness of breath
  • lung collapse

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden onset. LOCATION: Unilateral chest. DURATION: Acute, persistent until treated. CHARACTER: Sudden chest pain, dyspnea. AGGRAVATING: Exertion, lying flat. RELIEVING: Sitting upright, chest tube. TIMING: Acute, sudden. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (6-8/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Sudden chest pain, dyspnea, cough, anxiety. KEY POSITIVES: Sudden onset, chest pain, dyspnea. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no wheezing.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, acute, immediate
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: unilateral chest, one side, affected side
  Negative indicators: bilateral, both sides, diffuse

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: acute, persistent until treated, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: sudden chest pain, dyspnea
  Negative indicators: no fever, no wheezing, sharp, stabbing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, lying flat, activity
  Negative indicators: sitting upright, chest tube, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: sitting upright, chest tube, rest
  Negative indicators: exertion, lying flat, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, sudden, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 6-8/10, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Signs of tension pneumothorax

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Severe chest pain

  🚨 Signs of shock

  🚨 Mediastinal shift
