CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • abdominal pain
  • cramping
  • bloating
  • diarrhea
  • constipation

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: CHRONIC, RECURRENT pattern over months to years (NOT acute). Gradual development, waxing and waning. LOCATION: LOWER ABDOMEN, diffuse. Cramping migrates, not fixed to one spot. DURATION: CHRONIC condition lasting months to years. Individual cramping episodes last minutes to hours. Symptoms present at least once per week for 3+ months. CHARACTER: CRAMPY, INTERMITTENT discomfort. NOT sharp or severe. Bloating, gas sensations. AGGRAVATING: STRESS worsens symptoms significantly. Certain FOODS trigger (dairy, gluten, FODMAPs). Anxiety increases cramping. RELIEVING: DEFECATION provides relief. Stress reduction helps. Dietary changes improve. Antispasmodics reduce cramping. TIMING: INTERMITTENT cramping related to bowel movements. Pattern waxes and wanes. Often worse with stress. NO NOCTURNAL symptoms (doesn't wake from sleep). SEVERITY: 3-6/10, mild to moderate. Not severe enough to disrupt daily life significantly. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: BLOATING and gas, mucus in stool (NO blood), sense of incomplete evacuation, alternating diarrhea/constipation. KEY POSITIVES: Chronic >6 months, pain relief with defecation, stress/food triggers, NO alarm features (no blood, weight loss, fever). KEY NEGATIVES: NEW onset age >50 needs workup for cancer. BLOOD in stool rules out irritable bowel syndrome (IBS). Weight loss, fever, NOCTURNAL diarrhea suggests inflammatory bowel disease (IBD)/organic disease.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: chronic, months, years, recurrent
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, hours, new

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: lower abdomen, diffuse, crampy, not localized
  Negative indicators: right lower quadrant, RLQ, localized, point tender
  Anatomical type: diffuse

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic, months, years, episodic, minutes to hours
  Negative indicators: constant, days, persistent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: crampy, bloating, gas-like, pressure
  Negative indicators: sharp, stabbing, severe, knife-like

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: stress, certain foods, dairy, anxiety
  Negative indicators: movement, coughing, physical activity

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: defecation, bowel movement, passing gas
  Negative indicators: lying still, avoiding movement

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes, variable
  Negative indicators: constant, continuous, persistent

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: mild, moderate, 3, 4, 5, 6, 3-6/10
  Negative indicators: severe, 8, 9, 10, unbearable

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Blood in stool - needs colonoscopy to rule out inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) or colon cancer

  🚨 Unintentional weight loss - organic pathology, not irritable bowel syndrome (IBS)

  🚨 New onset after age 50 - higher risk of colon cancer, needs colonoscopy

  🚨 Nocturnal symptoms waking patient from sleep - suggests organic disease
