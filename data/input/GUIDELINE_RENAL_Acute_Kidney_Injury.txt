CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • acute kidney injury
  • kidney failure
  • renal failure
  • decreased urine output
  • swelling
  • fluid retention
  • kidney problems

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over hours to days. LOCATION: BILATERAL kidney involvement. DURATION: Develops over hours to days, may persist for days to weeks. CHARACTER: DECREASED urine output, swelling, fluid retention. AGGRAVATING: DEHYDRATION, medications, contrast agents. RELIEVING: FLUID management, dialysis if severe. TIMING: Gradual onset, develops over hours to days. SEVERITY: 6-9/10, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: DECREASED URINE OUTPUT, swelling, fluid retention, may have nausea, vomiting, confusion, may have electrolyte imbalances. KEY POSITIVES: Decreased urine output, swelling, fluid retention, elevated creatinine, elevated BUN. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, progressive, hours, days, weeks
  Negative indicators: sudden, immediate

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: kidney, renal, bilateral

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe, decreased urine output, swelling, fluid retention

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: dehydration, medications, contrast agents

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: fluid management, dialysis

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 6-9/10, moderate to severe

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe fluid overload with respiratory distress - possible pulmonary edema, call 911

  🚨 Signs of severe electrolyte imbalances - confusion, seizures, cardiac arrhythmias - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of uremia - confusion, seizures, coma - call 911

  🚨 Signs of severe metabolic acidosis - deep breathing, confusion, coma - call 911
