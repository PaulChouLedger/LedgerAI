PAUL CHOU — CEO & FOUNDER, LEDGER AI
======================================

BACKGROUND
Paul Chou is the founder and CEO of Ledger AI, a company building on-device AI assistants for healthcare and consumer applications. Based in the United States, Paul has a background in engineering and entrepreneurship. He is passionate about privacy-first AI — the belief that powerful AI should run locally on hardware the user owns, never sending data to the cloud.

COMPANY OVERVIEW
Ledger AI develops "Aura," a voice-first AI assistant that runs entirely on NVIDIA Jetson hardware. The device features a circular display (the "Puck"), a 4-microphone array for far-field voice capture, and local LLM inference using quantized Qwen models. The company's thesis is that personal AI must be private AI — no cloud, no data harvesting.

- Voice cloning: ChatterboxTTS voice cloning quality varies; exploring Kokoro as alternative
- Distribution: Need to decide between direct-to-consumer vs B2B healthcare sales channel
- Team: Currently a small team; Paul handles engineering, product, and business development

LEDGER AI TECHNICAL STACK
- Hardware: NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano, Seeed XVF3800 4-mic array, circular IPS display
- LLM: Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct (Q4_K_M quantized), with 7B model for deep thinking
- TTS: Kokoro-82M (primary), Chatterbox (voice cloning), ElevenLabs (cloud fallback)
- STT: faster-whisper running in Docker container on GPU
- Framework: PyQt5 circular GUI, Python backend, Docker orchestration
- Memory: FAISS-based semantic search for conversation history and RAG
