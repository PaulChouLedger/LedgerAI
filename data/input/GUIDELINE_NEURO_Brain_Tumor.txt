CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • brain tumor
  • brain cancer
  • headache
  • seizures
  • vision problems
  • cognitive changes
  • neurological symptoms

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over weeks to months. LOCATION: VARIABLE depending on tumor location. DURATION: Progressive over weeks to months. CHARACTER: VARIABLE symptoms. AGGRAVATING: ACTIVITY, stress, fatigue. RELIEVING: SUPPORTIVE care, medications. TIMING: Progressive over weeks to months. SEVERITY: 4-8/10, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: VARIABLE - headache, seizures, vision problems, cognitive changes, may have focal neurological deficits. KEY POSITIVES: Variable symptoms, may have MRI changes, may have focal neurological deficits. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no photophobia.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, weeks to months, slow onset, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate, hours

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: variable depending on tumor location, focal, specific area, localized
  Negative indicators: generalized, diffuse, bilateral, whole brain

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive over weeks to months, ongoing, persistent, worsening
  Negative indicators: acute, brief, transient, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: variable symptoms, moderate to severe, progressive, focal
  Negative indicators: mild, generalized, localized, acute

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: activity, stress, fatigue, exertion
  Negative indicators: supportive care, medications, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: supportive care, medications, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: activity, stress, fatigue, exertion

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive over weeks to months, worsening, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 4-8/10, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible severe brain tumor, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911
