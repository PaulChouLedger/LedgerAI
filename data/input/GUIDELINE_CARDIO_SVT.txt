Condition: Supraventricular Tachycardia (SVT)
Category: N/A

Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • palpitations
  • racing heart
  • fast heartbeat
  • heart pounding

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN, abrupt start ("like a switch flipped"). LOCATION: Chest, palpitations. DURATION: Minutes to hours. CHARACTER: REGULAR, RAPID palpitations, "racing" heart. AGGRAVATING: Caffeine, stress, exertion. RELIEVING: Vagal maneuvers (bearing down, carotid massage), sometimes spontaneous. TIMING: Paroxysmal (sudden start and stop). SEVERITY: Uncomfortable but not painful typically. KEY POSITIVES: Heart rate 150-250 bpm, regular rhythm, lightheadedness, dyspnea, chest pressure, sudden onset and offset. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT irregularly irregular (AFib), NOT gradual onset.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, abrupt start, like a switch flipped
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, palpitations
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, extremities

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: minutes to hours, variable
  Negative indicators: continuous, unrelenting, days

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: regular, rapid palpitations, racing heart
  Negative indicators: irregular, slow, steady

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: caffeine, stress, exertion
  Negative indicators: vagal maneuvers, bearing down, carotid massage

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: vagal maneuvers, bearing down, carotid massage, sometimes spontaneous
  Negative indicators: caffeine, stress, exertion

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: paroxysmal, sudden start and stop
  Negative indicators: constant, unrelenting, continuous

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: uncomfortable, not painful typically, moderate
  Negative indicators: severe, painful, mild

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Hemodynamic instability

  🚨 Syncope or near-syncope

  🚨 Chest pain

  🚨 Severe dyspnea

  🚨 Heart rate >200 bpm

  🚨 Signs of heart failure

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 History of Wolff-Parkinson-White syndrome (WPW) (Wolff-Parkinson-White)
