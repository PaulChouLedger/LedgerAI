Condition: Benign Arrhythmia (PAC/PVC)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: routine
Prevalence: common

SNOMED Code: 698247007
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • palpitations
  • heart skipping beats
  • fluttering in chest
  • irregular heartbeat

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Intermittent, episodic. LOCATION: Chest, precordial area. DURATION: Brief, seconds per episode. CHARACTER: "Skipped beat", "flip-flop", brief flutter or pause. AGGRAVATING: Caffeine, alcohol, stress, fatigue, lack of sleep. RELIEVING: Relaxation, reducing stimulants. TIMING: Intermittent, random. SEVERITY: Mild discomfort, more bothersome than painful. KEY POSITIVES: Brief episodes, no syncope, no dyspnea, otherwise healthy, triggered by caffeine or stress. KEY NEGATIVES: NO sustained rapid heart rate, NO syncope, NO chest pain, NO dyspnea.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: intermittent, episodic
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, continuous

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, precordial area
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, extremities

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: brief, seconds per episode, short duration
  Negative indicators: continuous, unrelenting, hours

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: skipped beat, flip-flop, brief flutter, pause
  Negative indicators: sustained rapid heart rate, syncope, chest pain, dyspnea

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: caffeine, alcohol, stress, fatigue, lack of sleep
  Negative indicators: relaxation, reducing stimulants, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: relaxation, reducing stimulants
  Negative indicators: caffeine, alcohol, stress, fatigue

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: intermittent, random
  Negative indicators: constant, unrelenting, continuous

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: mild discomfort, more bothersome than painful, mild
  Negative indicators: severe, painful, moderate

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Syncope or near-syncope

  🚨 Chest pain

  🚨 Severe dyspnea

  🚨 Sustained palpitations >30 seconds

  🚨 Family history of sudden cardiac death

  🚨 Structural heart disease
