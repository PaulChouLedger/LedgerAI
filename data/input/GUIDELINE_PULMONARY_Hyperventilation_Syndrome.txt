CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • hyperventilation
  • can't catch my breath
  • breathing too fast
  • panic attack
  • anxiety breathing

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden, often triggered by stress or anxiety. LOCATION: Diffuse chest. DURATION: Minutes to hours. CHARACTER: Dyspnea, chest tightness, 'can't get enough air'. AGGRAVATING: Stress, anxiety, panic. RELIEVING: Breathing exercises, paper bag breathing, reassurance. TIMING: Episodic, often during stress. SEVERITY: Moderate (5-7/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Paresthesias, dizziness, lightheadedness, chest tightness, palpitations, anxiety. KEY POSITIVES: Anxiety, stress, paresthesias, dizziness. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no chest pain (unless secondary), no wheezing.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, often triggered by stress or anxiety, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: diffuse chest
  Negative indicators: localized, one side only, specific point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: minutes to hours, episodic, variable
  Negative indicators: chronic, persistent, ongoing

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: dyspnea, chest tightness, can't get enough air
  Negative indicators: no fever, no chest pain, no wheezing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: stress, anxiety, panic
  Negative indicators: breathing exercises, paper bag breathing, reassurance

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: breathing exercises, paper bag breathing, reassurance
  Negative indicators: stress, anxiety, panic

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: episodic, often during stress, intermittent
  Negative indicators: continuous, persistent, ongoing

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate, 5-7/10, 5, 6, 7
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 8-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Chest pain with breathing

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Unconsciousness

  🚨 Severe anxiety or panic

  🚨 Underlying cardiac or pulmonary disease
