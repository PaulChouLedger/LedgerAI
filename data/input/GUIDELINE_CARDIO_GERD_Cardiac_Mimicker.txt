--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chest burning
  • chest pain
  • heartburn
  • acid reflux

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual, often post-prandial (after eating). LOCATION: RETROSTERNAL, epigastric, can mimic cardiac pain. RADIATES upward to throat. DURATION: Minutes to hours. CHARACTER: BURNING, rising sensation from stomach. AGGRAVATING: Large meals, fatty foods, spicy foods, lying flat, bending over. RELIEVING: Antacids, sitting upright, PPIs. TIMING: Postprandial, worse at night when lying down. SEVERITY: 3-6/10. KEY POSITIVES: Sour taste, regurgitation, worse after meals, worse lying flat, chronic history, relieved by antacids. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT exertional, NOT radiating to arm/jaw (though can), NOT diaphoresis.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, post-prandial, after eating
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: retrosternal, epigastric, can mimic cardiac pain, radiates upward to throat
  Negative indicators: radiates to arm, radiates to jaw, radiates to back

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: minutes to hours, variable
  Negative indicators: continuous, unrelenting, days

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: burning, rising sensation from stomach
  Negative indicators: crushing, pressure, sharp, stabbing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: large meals, fatty foods, spicy foods, lying flat, bending over
  Negative indicators: antacids, sitting upright, PPIs

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: antacids, sitting upright, PPIs
  Negative indicators: large meals, fatty foods, spicy foods, lying flat

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: postprandial, worse at night, worse lying down
  Negative indicators: exertional, at rest, unpredictable

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 3-6/10, 3, 4, 5, 6
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-2/10, severe, 7-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 New onset in older patient (rule out cardiac)

  🚨 Radiation to arm or jaw (atypical for gastroesophageal reflux disease )

  🚨 Exertional pattern (suggests cardiac)

  🚨 Diaphoresis or dyspnea

  🚨 No relief with antacids

  🚨 Cardiac risk factors present
