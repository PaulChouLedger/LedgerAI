CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • cystic fibrosis
  • CF
  • chronic lung disease
  • recurrent lung infections
  • thick mucus

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Chronic from birth, acute exacerbations. LOCATION: Bilateral, diffuse. DURATION: Chronic with acute exacerbations. CHARACTER: Chronic productive cough, dyspnea. AGGRAVATING: Infections, thick secretions, dehydration. RELIEVING: Chest physiotherapy, mucolytics, antibiotics. TIMING: Chronic baseline with acute worsening. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (5-9/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Chronic productive cough, recurrent infections, dyspnea, failure to thrive, salty skin, clubbing, sinusitis. KEY POSITIVES: Family history, chronic cough, recurrent infections, failure to thrive. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain (unless infection), no wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: chronic from birth, acute exacerbations, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral, diffuse
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic with acute exacerbations, ongoing, persistent
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: chronic productive cough, dyspnea
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, dry cough

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: infections, thick secretions, dehydration
  Negative indicators: chest physiotherapy, mucolytics, antibiotics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: chest physiotherapy, mucolytics, antibiotics
  Negative indicators: infections, thick secretions, dehydration

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic baseline with acute worsening, continuous, persistent
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 5-9/10, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe respiratory distress

  🚨 High fever with purulent sputum

  🚨 Hemoptysis

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Cyanosis

  🚨 Weight loss or failure to thrive

  🚨 Recurrent pneumonias
