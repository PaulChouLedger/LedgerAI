CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • epiglottitis
  • throat swelling
  • stridor
  • difficulty swallowing
  • sore throat with breathing problems

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Rapid onset over hours. LOCATION: Supraglottic region. DURATION: Acute, hours to days. CHARACTER: Severe throat pain, dysphagia, dyspnea. AGGRAVATING: Swallowing, lying flat. RELIEVING: Sitting upright, humidified air, antibiotics. TIMING: Acute, progressive. SEVERITY: Severe (8-10/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: High fever, drooling, stridor, dysphagia, muffled voice, tripod positioning, anxiety. KEY POSITIVES: High fever, drooling, stridor, dysphagia, tripod positioning. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain, no wheezing.

N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: rapid onset over hours, acute, immediate
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: supraglottic region, throat, upper airway
  Negative indicators: lower airway, chest, lungs

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: acute, hours to days, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe throat pain, dysphagia, dyspnea
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: swallowing, lying flat, activity
  Negative indicators: sitting upright, humidified air, antibiotics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: sitting upright, humidified air, antibiotics
  Negative indicators: swallowing, lying flat, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, progressive, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Stridor or respiratory distress

  🚨 Drooling or inability to swallow

  🚨 High fever >102°F

  🚨 Tripod positioning

  🚨 Muffled voice or 'hot potato' voice

  🚨 Cyanosis

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Signs of airway obstruction
