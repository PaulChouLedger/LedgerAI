CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • sleep apnea
  • snoring
  • sleep problems
  • fatigue
  • daytime sleepiness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual over months to years. LOCATION: Upper airway. DURATION: Chronic, persistent. CHARACTER: Episodic breathing cessation during sleep. AGGRAVATING: Supine position, alcohol, sedatives. RELIEVING: CPAP, weight loss, positioning. TIMING: Nocturnal, episodic. SEVERITY: Mild to moderate (3-6/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Loud snoring, witnessed apneas, daytime sleepiness, fatigue, morning headaches. KEY POSITIVES: Loud snoring, witnessed apneas, daytime sleepiness. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain, no wheezing.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual over months to years, progressive, slow onset
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: upper airway
  Negative indicators: lower airway, chest, lungs

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic, persistent, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: episodic breathing cessation during sleep, loud snoring, witnessed apneas
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, continuous

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: supine position, alcohol, sedatives
  Negative indicators: CPAP, weight loss, positioning

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: CPAP, weight loss, positioning
  Negative indicators: supine position, alcohol, sedatives

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: nocturnal, episodic, during sleep
  Negative indicators: continuous, daytime, awake

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: mild to moderate, 3-6/10, 3, 4, 5, 6
  Negative indicators: severe, 7-10/10, mild, 1-2/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe daytime sleepiness

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Severe fatigue

  🚨 Signs of cardiac complications

  🚨 Severe snoring with witnessed apneas

  🚨 Signs of hypoxemia

  🚨 Signs of pulmonary hypertension
