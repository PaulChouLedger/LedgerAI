CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • meningitis
  • headache
  • neck stiffness
  • fever
  • photophobia
  • nausea
  • vomiting

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within hours to days. LOCATION: GENERALIZED symptoms. DURATION: Develops over hours to days. CHARACTER: SEVERE headache, neck stiffness, fever. AGGRAVATING: LIGHT, sound, movement. RELIEVING: ANTIBIOTICS, supportive care. TIMING: Sudden onset, develops over hours to days. SEVERITY: 8-10/10, severe and unrelenting. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: SEVERE HEADACHE, neck stiffness, fever, photophobia, nausea, vomiting, may have altered mental status. KEY POSITIVES: Severe headache, neck stiffness, fever, photophobia, positive Kernig sign, positive Brudzinski sign. KEY NEGATIVES: No focal neurological deficits.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, within hours to days, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset, progressive

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized, diffuse, whole head, neck
  Negative indicators: localized, focal, specific point, unilateral

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: develops over hours to days, progressive, ongoing, persistent
  Negative indicators: minutes, seconds, brief, transient

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe headache, neck stiffness, fever, severe and unrelenting
  Negative indicators: mild, focal deficits, localized, moderate

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: light, sound, movement, stimuli
  Negative indicators: antibiotics, supportive care, dark, quiet

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: antibiotics, supportive care, dark, quiet
  Negative indicators: light, sound, movement, stimuli

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, progressive, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe and unrelenting, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate, variable

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible severe meningitis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911
