CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • pulmonary edema
  • fluid in lungs
  • shortness of breath
  • heart failure
  • can't breathe

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden or gradual over hours. LOCATION: Bilateral, diffuse. DURATION: Hours to days. CHARACTER: Severe dyspnea, 'drowning sensation'. AGGRAVATING: Lying flat, exertion. RELIEVING: Sitting upright, oxygen, diuretics. TIMING: Acute, progressive. SEVERITY: Severe (8-10/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Severe dyspnea, orthopnea, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, pink frothy sputum, anxiety. KEY POSITIVES: Severe dyspnea, orthopnea, pink frothy sputum. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain (unless secondary), no wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden or gradual over hours, acute, progressive
  Negative indicators: chronic, slow onset, gradual over days

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral, diffuse
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: hours to days, acute, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe dyspnea, drowning sensation
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: lying flat, exertion, activity
  Negative indicators: sitting upright, oxygen, diuretics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: sitting upright, oxygen, diuretics
  Negative indicators: lying flat, exertion, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, progressive, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Pink frothy sputum

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Signs of shock

  🚨 Severe anxiety or panic

  🚨 Inability to speak
