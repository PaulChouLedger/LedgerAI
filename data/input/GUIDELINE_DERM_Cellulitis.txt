CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • skin infection
  • cellulitis
  • red swollen skin
  • hot skin
  • skin abscess
  • infected wound
  • skin redness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over 24-48 hours, often following skin break (cut, scrape, insect bite). LOCATION: Lower extremities most common, but can occur anywhere. DURATION: Progressive over 24-48 hours, may worsen rapidly. CHARACTER: ERYTHEMATOUS (red), WARM, TENDER, EDEMATOUS (swollen) skin. AGGRAVATING: TOUCH, pressure, movement. RELIEVING: ELEVATION, cool compresses, antibiotics. TIMING: Progressive over 24-48 hours, may spread rapidly. SEVERITY: 5-9/10 pain, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: FEVER (100-102°F), chills, malaise, lymphangitis (red streaks), lymphadenopathy (swollen lymph nodes), may have purulent drainage. KEY POSITIVES: Erythema, warmth, tenderness, edema, fever, lymphangitis. KEY NEGATIVES: No crepitus (rules out necrotizing fasciitis), no bullae or skin necrosis, no systemic shock.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, 24-48 hours, following skin break, cut, scrape, insect bite
  Negative indicators: sudden, immediate, chronic

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: lower extremities, anywhere, skin, cutaneous
  Negative indicators: internal organs, deep tissues

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive, 24-48 hours, may worsen rapidly
  Negative indicators: chronic, months, years

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: erythematous, red, warm, tender, edematous, swollen
  Negative indicators: pale, cool, non-tender, normal skin

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: touch, pressure, movement
  Negative indicators: rest, immobilization

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: elevation, cool compresses, antibiotics
  Negative indicators: heat, massage, movement

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive, 24-48 hours, may spread rapidly
  Negative indicators: constant, intermittent, chronic

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 5-9/10, moderate, severe
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, asymptomatic

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 High fever >102°F with rigors - possible sepsis, call 911

  🚨 Rapidly spreading redness with severe pain - possible necrotizing fasciitis, call 911

  🚨 Bullae, skin necrosis, or crepitus - necrotizing fasciitis, call 911

  🚨 Severe systemic symptoms - hypotension, tachycardia, altered mental status - sepsis, call 911

  🚨 Red streaks extending from infection site - lymphangitis, needs urgent evaluation
