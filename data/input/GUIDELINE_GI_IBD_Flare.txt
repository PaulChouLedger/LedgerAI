CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • abdominal pain
  • diarrhea
  • bloody diarrhea
  • cramping

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL over days to weeks. Progressive worsening. Patient usually has KNOWN inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) diagnosis with prior flares. LOCATION: Diffuse cramping throughout abdomen, or right lower quadrant (RLQ) if Crohn's (terminal ileum involved). DURATION: Flares last days to weeks. Cramping comes in waves, diarrhea persists. Chronic relapsing pattern. CHARACTER: CRAMPY ABDOMINAL PAIN. Intermittent, wave-like cramping with bowel movements. AGGRAVATING: EATING may worsen cramping. Stress can trigger flares. Missed medications worsen. RELIEVING: Defecation provides TEMPORARY relief of cramping. Anti-inflammatory medications (steroids, biologics) needed for flare resolution. TIMING: INTERMITTENT cramping with bowel movements. Diarrhea is frequent (3-10+ stools/day). Pattern fluctuates. SEVERITY: 5-8/10 cramping, moderate. Not as severe as surgical emergencies. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: CHRONIC BLOODY OR MUCOID DIARRHEA (hallmark), urgency, tenesmus, weight loss, fatigue, LOW-GRADE FEVER. May have extra-intestinal symptoms (joint pain, eye inflammation). KEY POSITIVES: Blood/mucus in stool, cramping with BMs, chronic relapsing pattern, known inflammatory bowel disease (IBD) diagnosis. KEY NEGATIVES: ACUTE onset without blood suggests gastroenteritis. FIRST episode bloody diarrhea needs workup (not assumed inflammatory bowel disease ).

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: everywhere, all over, RLQ, diffuse, right lower quadrant, McBurney, right lower

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe abdominal pain with peritoneal signs - toxic megacolon or perforation, call 911

  🚨 High fever >102°F with tachycardia - severe colitis, toxic megacolon risk

  🚨 Severe bloody diarrhea with >10 stools/day - severe flare, needs hospitalization

  🚨 Abdominal distension with decreased bowel sounds - toxic megacolon, surgical emergency
