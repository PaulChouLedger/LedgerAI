CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • shoulder pain
  • rotator cuff injury
  • shoulder tear
  • can't lift arm
  • shoulder weakness
  • shoulder impingement
  • shoulder bursitis

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over weeks to months (chronic) or SUDDEN onset during trauma (acute). Chronic from repetitive overhead activities, acute from fall or direct blow. LOCATION: Anterior shoulder, may radiate to upper arm. DURATION: Chronic pain lasting weeks to months, or acute pain lasting days to weeks. CHARACTER: DULL, ACHING pain with sharp episodes. AGGRAVATING: OVERHEAD activities, reaching behind back, sleeping on affected side, lifting, pushing, pulling. RELIEVING: REST, ice, NSAIDs, physical therapy. TIMING: Pain worse at night, especially when sleeping on affected side. SEVERITY: 4-7/10, moderate to severe with activity. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: WEAKNESS with overhead activities, difficulty reaching behind back, night pain, stiffness, may have 'catching' or 'locking' sensation. KEY POSITIVES: Positive impingement signs, weakness with external rotation, positive drop arm test, positive empty can test. KEY NEGATIVES: No obvious deformity, no neurological deficits, no fever.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, weeks to months, sudden, during trauma, fall, direct blow
  Negative indicators: immediate, seconds, minutes, hours

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: anterior shoulder, upper arm, shoulder, shoulder joint
  Negative indicators: elbow, wrist, neck, chest

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: weeks to months, days to weeks, chronic, persistent
  Negative indicators: minutes, hours, brief, transient

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: dull, aching, sharp episodes, moderate
  Negative indicators: severe, constant, burning, numbness

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: overhead activities, reaching behind back, sleeping on affected side, lifting, pushing, pulling
  Negative indicators: rest, ice, NSAIDs, physical therapy

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: rest, ice, NSAIDs, physical therapy, treatment
  Negative indicators: overhead activities, reaching behind back, sleeping on affected side, lifting

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: worse at night, sleeping on affected side, chronic, persistent
  Negative indicators: morning only, daytime only, intermittent, variable

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 4-7/10, 4, 5, 6, 7
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, severe, 8-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe pain with sudden weakness - possible acute rotator cuff tear, needs urgent evaluation

  🚨 Severe pain with fever - possible infection, needs urgent evaluation

  🚨 Severe pain with neurological deficits - possible nerve injury, needs urgent evaluation

  🚨 Severe pain with night pain - possible malignancy, needs urgent evaluation

  🚨 Severe pain with weight loss - possible malignancy, needs urgent evaluation
