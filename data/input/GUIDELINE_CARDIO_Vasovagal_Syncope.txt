Condition: Vasovagal Syncope (Fainting)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: routine
Prevalence: common
ICD-10 Code: R55
SNOMED Code: 398036000
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • fainted
  • passed out
  • lost consciousness
  • syncope
  • lightheadedness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden, preceded by prodrome (warning signs). LOCATION: Generalized, loss of consciousness. DURATION: Seconds to 1-2 minutes, brief. CHARACTER: Lightheadedness, tunnel vision, nausea, sweating before fainting. AGGRAVATING: Prolonged standing, heat, dehydration, pain, emotional stress, sight of blood. RELIEVING: Lying down, recovery rapid once horizontal. TIMING: Single episode or rare episodes with clear triggers. SEVERITY: Complete LOC but brief. KEY POSITIVES: Prodrome (warning), rapid recovery, no confusion post-event, clear trigger, young patient. KEY NEGATIVES: NO exertional syncope (cardiac), NO chest pain, NO palpitations before event, NO seizure activity.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, preceded by prodrome, warning signs
  Negative indicators: gradual, no warning, chronic

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized, loss of consciousness
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, chest

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: seconds to 1-2 minutes, brief, short
  Negative indicators: prolonged, >5 minutes, hours

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: lightheadedness, tunnel vision, nausea, sweating before fainting
  Negative indicators: chest pain, palpitations, seizure activity

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: prolonged standing, heat, dehydration, pain, emotional stress, sight of blood
  Negative indicators: lying down, rest, medication

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: lying down, recovery rapid once horizontal
  Negative indicators: prolonged standing, heat, dehydration

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: single episode, rare episodes, clear triggers
  Negative indicators: frequent, unpredictable, no triggers

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: complete LOC, brief, rapid recovery
  Negative indicators: prolonged, confusion post-event, severe

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Exertional syncope (suggests cardiac cause)

  🚨 No prodrome (sudden without warning)

  🚨 Prolonged loss of consciousness >5 minutes

  🚨 Chest pain or palpitations before syncope

  🚨 Family history of sudden cardiac death

  🚨 Age >60 years

  🚨 Injury from fall

  🚨 Seizure-like activity
