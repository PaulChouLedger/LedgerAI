Condition: Takotsubo Cardiomyopathy (Broken Heart Syndrome)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: urgent
Prevalence: rare
ICD-10 Code: I51.81
SNOMED Code: 418304008
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chest pain
  • shortness of breath
  • chest pain after stress

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Acute, often after EMOTIONAL or PHYSICAL STRESS. LOCATION: Chest (retrosternal). RADIATES to arm, jaw. DURATION: Minutes to hours. CHARACTER: Chest pain, pressure, dyspnea mimicking myocardial infarction (MI). AGGRAVATING: Recent severe emotional stress (death of loved one, bad news, argument). RELIEVING: Supportive care. TIMING: Acute onset after stressor. SEVERITY: 7-9/10. KEY POSITIVES: Postmenopausal woman, recent severe stress or emotional event, electrocardiogram (ECG) mimics myocardial infarction (MI) (ST elevation or T-wave inversion), normal coronaries on angiography, apical ballooning on echo. KEY NEGATIVES: NO significant coronary artery disease (differentiates from true myocardial infarction ).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: acute, often after emotional stress, often after physical stress
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, retrosternal, radiates to arm, radiates to jaw
  Negative indicators: right side only, no radiation, localized point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: minutes to hours, brief
  Negative indicators: continuous, unrelenting, days

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: chest pain, pressure, dyspnea mimicking myocardial infarction
  Negative indicators: sharp, stabbing, burning, no pain

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: recent severe emotional stress, death of loved one, bad news, argument
  Negative indicators: supportive care, medication, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: supportive care
  Negative indicators: emotional stress, physical stress, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute onset after stressor, sudden
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 7-9/10, 7, 8, 9
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-6/10, severe, 10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe chest pain mimicking myocardial infarction (MI)

  🚨 ST elevation on electrocardiogram (ECG)

  🚨 Troponin elevation (mild)

  🚨 Heart failure symptoms

  🚨 Arrhythmias

  🚨 Cardiogenic shock

  🚨 Recent severe emotional stress
