CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • mini stroke
  • TIA
  • transient weakness
  • temporary numbness
  • speech difficulty
  • vision problems
  • dizziness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within minutes. LOCATION: UNILATERAL weakness, numbness, or paralysis. DURATION: SYMPTOMS RESOLVE within 24 hours (usually within minutes to hours). CHARACTER: SUDDEN, SEVERE weakness, numbness, or paralysis that RESOLVES. AGGRAVATING: MOVEMENT, speaking, swallowing. RELIEVING: SYMPTOMS RESOLVE spontaneously. TIMING: Sudden onset, symptoms resolve within 24 hours. SEVERITY: 6-8/10 during episode, then resolves. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: FACIAL DROOP, arm weakness, speech difficulty, vision problems, dizziness, confusion, headache. KEY POSITIVES: Positive FAST test (Face, Arm, Speech, Time), unilateral weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no photophobia.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, within minutes, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset, progressive

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: unilateral weakness, numbness, paralysis, one side
  Negative indicators: bilateral, both sides, generalized, diffuse

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: resolves within 24 hours, minutes to hours, transient, brief
  Negative indicators: persistent, ongoing, chronic, permanent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: sudden, severe weakness, numbness, paralysis that resolves
  Negative indicators: mild, gradual, persistent, permanent

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: movement, speaking, swallowing, activity
  Negative indicators: symptoms resolve spontaneously, rest, treatment, medications

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: symptoms resolve spontaneously, rest, treatment, medications
  Negative indicators: movement, speaking, swallowing, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, resolves within 24 hours, transient
  Negative indicators: continuous, persistent, unrelenting

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 6-8/10 during episode, 6, 7, 8, moderate to severe during episode
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Symptoms lasting >24 hours - possible stroke, call 911

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible stroke, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911
