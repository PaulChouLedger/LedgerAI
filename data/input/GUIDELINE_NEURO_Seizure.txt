CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • seizure
  • convulsion
  • epilepsy
  • shaking
  • unconscious
  • jerking
  • fainting

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within seconds. LOCATION: GENERALIZED or focal. DURATION: Lasts seconds to minutes. CHARACTER: INVOLUNTARY movements, jerking, convulsions. AGGRAVATING: STRESS, sleep deprivation, alcohol withdrawal. RELIEVING: ANTICONVULSANTS, supportive care. TIMING: Sudden onset, lasts seconds to minutes. SEVERITY: 8-10/10 during episode, then resolves. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: LOSS OF CONSCIOUSNESS, jerking movements, tongue biting, incontinence, postictal confusion. KEY POSITIVES: Involuntary movements, loss of consciousness, postictal confusion, tongue biting. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no photophobia.

N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, within seconds, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset, progressive

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized, focal, whole body, specific area
  Negative indicators: localized, specific point, unilateral only

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: seconds to minutes, brief, transient, short
  Negative indicators: hours, days, persistent, ongoing

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: involuntary movements, jerking, convulsions, severe during episode
  Negative indicators: voluntary, controlled, mild, localized

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: stress, sleep deprivation, alcohol withdrawal, triggers
  Negative indicators: anticonvulsants, supportive care, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: anticonvulsants, supportive care, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: stress, sleep deprivation, alcohol withdrawal, triggers

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, episodic, intermittent
  Negative indicators: continuous, persistent, unrelenting

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 8-10/10 during episode, 8, 9, 10, severe during episode
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate, constant

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Seizure lasting >5 minutes - status epilepticus, call 911

  🚨 Multiple seizures without regaining consciousness - status epilepticus, call 911

  🚨 Seizure with severe head injury - possible traumatic brain injury, call 911

  🚨 Seizure with signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Seizure with severe hypertension - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911
