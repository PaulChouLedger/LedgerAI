--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • tearing chest pain
  • ripping chest pain
  • sudden severe chest pain
  • back pain radiating

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN, instantaneous, maximal at onset ("like a light switch"). LOCATION: ANTERIOR CHEST (Type A) or INTERSCAPULAR BACK (Type B). RADIATES down back as dissection propagates, can radiate to abdomen. DURATION: Continuous, persistent. CHARACTER: TEARING, RIPPING, SHARP, most severe pain ever experienced. AGGRAVATING: None (unprovoked). RELIEVING: Nothing helps. TIMING: Sudden onset, persistent. SEVERITY: 10/10, catastrophic pain. KEY POSITIVES: Pulse differential (unequal pulses between arms), blood pressure differential (>20 mmHg between arms), neurologic deficits, hypertension history, Marfan syndrome. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT gradual onset, NOT pressure/squeezing like myocardial infarction (MI).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, instantaneous, maximal at onset, like a light switch
  Negative indicators: gradual onset, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: anterior chest, Type A, interscapular back, Type B, radiates down back, can radiate to abdomen
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, no radiation

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: continuous, persistent, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: tearing, ripping, sharp, most severe pain ever experienced
  Negative indicators: pressure, squeezing, like myocardial infarction, dull

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: none, unprovoked
  Negative indicators: nothing helps, medication, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: nothing helps
  Negative indicators: medication, rest, position

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, persistent, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 10/10, catastrophic pain, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, 1-9/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Sudden maximal pain at onset

  🚨 Tearing or ripping quality

  🚨 Pulse differential between arms

  🚨 Blood pressure differential >20 mmHg

  🚨 Neurologic symptoms (stroke-like)

  🚨 History of hypertension

  🚨 Marfan syndrome or connective tissue disorder

  🚨 Syncope

  🚨 Aortic regurgitation murmur
