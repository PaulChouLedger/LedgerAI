CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • huntingtons disease
  • huntingtons
  • chorea
  • involuntary movements
  • cognitive decline
  • behavioral changes
  • family history

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over months to years. LOCATION: GENERALIZED symptoms. DURATION: Chronic progressive condition. CHARACTER: CHOREA, cognitive decline, behavioral changes. AGGRAVATING: STRESS, fatigue, medication changes. RELIEVING: SUPPORTIVE care, medications. TIMING: Chronic progressive, symptoms worsen over time. SEVERITY: 3-7/10, mild to moderate. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: CHOREA, cognitive decline, behavioral changes, may have psychiatric symptoms. KEY POSITIVES: Chorea, cognitive decline, behavioral changes, family history. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible severe Huntington's flare, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911
