CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • concussion
  • head injury
  • head trauma
  • brain injury
  • headache after fall
  • dizziness after injury
  • memory problems

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset after head trauma. LOCATION: GENERALIZED symptoms. DURATION: Symptoms may persist for days to weeks. CHARACTER: HEADACHE, dizziness, confusion. AGGRAVATING: LIGHT, sound, movement, cognitive tasks. RELIEVING: REST, avoiding triggers. TIMING: Immediate onset after trauma, may persist for days to weeks. SEVERITY: 4-8/10, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: HEADACHE, dizziness, confusion, memory problems, nausea, vomiting, sensitivity to light and sound. KEY POSITIVES: Headache, dizziness, confusion, memory problems, sensitivity to light and sound. KEY NEGATIVES: No focal neurological deficits, no signs of increased intracranial pressure.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, after head trauma, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset, progressive

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: generalized, diffuse, whole head, bilateral
  Negative indicators: localized, focal, specific point, unilateral

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: days to weeks, persistent, prolonged, ongoing
  Negative indicators: minutes, hours, brief, transient

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: headache, dizziness, confusion, moderate to severe
  Negative indicators: mild, focal deficits, severe neurological, localized

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: light, sound, movement, cognitive tasks
  Negative indicators: rest, avoiding triggers, dark, quiet

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: rest, avoiding triggers, dark, quiet
  Negative indicators: light, sound, movement, cognitive tasks

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: immediate onset after trauma, persistent, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 4-8/10, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe headache with vomiting - possible intracranial hemorrhage, call 911

  🚨 Altered mental status with focal neurological deficits - possible traumatic brain injury, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911
