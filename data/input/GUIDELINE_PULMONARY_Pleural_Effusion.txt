CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • pleural effusion
  • fluid in lungs
  • chest pain
  • shortness of breath
  • pleural fluid

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual over days to weeks. LOCATION: Unilateral or bilateral chest. DURATION: Days to weeks, can be chronic. CHARACTER: Dyspnea, chest discomfort. AGGRAVATING: Lying flat, exertion. RELIEVING: Sitting upright, thoracentesis. TIMING: Chronic, persistent. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (5-8/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Dyspnea, chest pain, cough, fever (if infectious), weight loss (if malignant). KEY POSITIVES: Dyspnea, chest pain, cough, fever. KEY NEGATIVES: No wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual over days to weeks, progressive, slow onset
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: unilateral or bilateral chest, one side, both sides
  Negative indicators: localized, specific point, diffuse

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: days to weeks, can be chronic, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: dyspnea, chest discomfort
  Negative indicators: no wheezing, sharp, stabbing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: lying flat, exertion, activity
  Negative indicators: sitting upright, thoracentesis, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: sitting upright, thoracentesis, rest
  Negative indicators: lying flat, exertion, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic, persistent, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 5-8/10, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Severe chest pain

  🚨 Signs of cardiac tamponade

  🚨 Hemoptysis

  🚨 Signs of shock
