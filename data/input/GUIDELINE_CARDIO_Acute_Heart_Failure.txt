CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • shortness of breath
  • difficulty breathing
  • can't breathe
  • chest tightness
  • leg swelling

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Hours to days, progressive worsening. LOCATION: Chest (dyspnea), bilateral lower extremities (edema). DURATION: Progressive, continuous dyspnea. CHARACTER: Dyspnea, orthopnea (can't lie flat), paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea (wake up gasping). AGGRAVATING: Lying flat, exertion, fluid intake. RELIEVING: Sitting upright, diuretics. TIMING: Progressive, worse at night. SEVERITY: Severe dyspnea, patient speaks in short phrases. KEY POSITIVES: Bilateral leg swelling, weight gain, pink frothy sputum, rales on lung exam, elevated JVP, S3 gallop. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT unilateral leg swelling (DVT), NOT pleuritic (PE).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: hours to days, progressive worsening, gradual
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, dyspnea, bilateral lower extremities, edema
  Negative indicators: unilateral leg swelling, localized, one area only

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive, continuous dyspnea, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: dyspnea, orthopnea, can't lie flat, paroxysmal nocturnal dyspnea, wake up gasping
  Negative indicators: pleuritic, sharp, stabbing, no respiratory symptoms

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: lying flat, exertion, fluid intake
  Negative indicators: sitting upright, diuretics, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: sitting upright, diuretics
  Negative indicators: lying flat, exertion, fluid intake

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive, worse at night, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe dyspnea, patient speaks in short phrases, severe
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, no symptoms

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Unable to lie flat (orthopnea)

  🚨 Pink frothy sputum

  🚨 Severe dyspnea at rest

  🚨 Hypoxia (low oxygen saturation)

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Hypotension

  🚨 Rapid weight gain (>5 lbs in days)

  🚨 History of heart failure or myocardial infarction (MI)
