CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • dialysis complications
  • dialysis problems
  • dialysis access
  • dialysis infection
  • dialysis bleeding
  • dialysis clotting
  • dialysis issues

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within minutes to hours. LOCATION: DIALYSIS access site or systemic. DURATION: Develops over minutes to hours, may persist for days to weeks. CHARACTER: ACCESS site problems, bleeding, clotting, infection. AGGRAVATING: DIALYSIS, movement. RELIEVING: ACCESS care, medications. TIMING: Sudden onset, develops over minutes to hours. SEVERITY: 6-9/10, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: ACCESS SITE PROBLEMS, bleeding, clotting, infection, may have fever, may have swelling. KEY POSITIVES: Access site problems, bleeding, clotting, infection, fever. KEY NEGATIVES: No neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe bleeding from access site - possible arterial bleeding, call 911

  🚨 Signs of severe infection - fever, chills, rigors - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of severe metabolic acidosis - deep breathing, confusion, coma - call 911

  🚨 Signs of severe electrolyte imbalances - confusion, seizures, cardiac arrhythmias - call 911
