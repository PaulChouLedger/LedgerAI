CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD)
  • emphysema
  • chronic bronchitis
  • shortness of breath
  • smoking-related lung disease

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual over years, acute exacerbations. LOCATION: Bilateral, diffuse. DURATION: Chronic with acute exacerbations lasting days to weeks. CHARACTER: Dyspnea, chest tightness, productive cough. AGGRAVATING: Infections, cold air, exertion, smoking. RELIEVING: Bronchodilators, oxygen, steroids, antibiotics. TIMING: Chronic baseline with acute worsening. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (5-9/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Chronic productive cough, dyspnea, wheezing, barrel chest, clubbing, weight loss, fatigue. KEY POSITIVES: Smoking history, chronic cough, dyspnea, wheezing. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain (unless pneumothorax), no fever (unless infection).

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual over years, acute exacerbations, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral, diffuse
  Negative indicators: localized, one side only, specific point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic with acute exacerbations, lasting days to weeks, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: dyspnea, chest tightness, productive cough
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no fever, sharp, stabbing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: infections, cold air, exertion, smoking
  Negative indicators: bronchodilators, oxygen, steroids, antibiotics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: bronchodilators, oxygen, steroids, antibiotics
  Negative indicators: infections, cold air, exertion, smoking

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic baseline with acute worsening, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 5-9/10, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea at rest

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Use of accessory muscles

  🚨 Inability to speak in full sentences

  🚨 High fever with purulent sputum

  🚨 Hemoptysis

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure
