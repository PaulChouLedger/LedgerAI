--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chest pain
  • chest wall pain
  • pain when breathing
  • rib pain

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual or after physical activity, coughing, or trauma. LOCATION: ANTERIOR CHEST WALL, costochondral junctions (where ribs meet cartilage), often left-sided. DURATION: Days to weeks. CHARACTER: SHARP, stabbing, localized. AGGRAVATING: Deep breathing, coughing, palpation of affected area, arm movements, twisting. RELIEVING: Rest, NSAIDs, avoiding movements. TIMING: Intermittent, worse with movements. SEVERITY: 4-7/10. KEY POSITIVES: REPRODUCIBLE with palpation (tender to touch), young patient, recent URI or cough, musculoskeletal pattern. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT radiating to arm/jaw, NOT exertional, NOT diaphoresis, NOT dyspnea.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, after physical activity, after coughing, after trauma
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: anterior chest wall, costochondral junctions, where ribs meet cartilage, often left-sided
  Negative indicators: retrosternal, radiates to arm, radiates to jaw, posterior

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: days to weeks, persistent
  Negative indicators: hours, minutes, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: sharp, stabbing, localized
  Negative indicators: crushing, pressure, dull, diffuse

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: deep breathing, coughing, palpation, arm movements, twisting
  Negative indicators: rest, NSAIDs, avoiding movements

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: rest, NSAIDs, avoiding movements
  Negative indicators: deep breathing, coughing, palpation, arm movements

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: intermittent, worse with movements
  Negative indicators: constant, continuous, unrelenting

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 4-7/10, 4, 5, 6, 7
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, severe, 8-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Fever (suggests infection)

  🚨 Cardiac risk factors present

  🚨 Radiation to arm or jaw (suggests cardiac)

  🚨 Dyspnea

  🚨 Diaphoresis

  🚨 Not reproducible with palpation (suggests cardiac)
