CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • pneumonia
  • chest infection
  • fever and cough
  • lung infection
  • shortness of breath

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual over days. LOCATION: Focal or diffuse lung areas. DURATION: Days to weeks. CHARACTER: Productive cough, chest pain, dyspnea. AGGRAVATING: Lying flat, exertion. RELIEVING: Antibiotics, oxygen, positioning. TIMING: Acute, persistent. SEVERITY: Moderate to severe (6-8/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: High fever, productive cough, chest pain, dyspnea, fatigue, malaise. KEY POSITIVES: High fever, productive cough, chest pain, dyspnea. KEY NEGATIVES: No wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, over days, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: focal lung, diffuse lung, lung areas, chest
  Negative indicators: abdomen, extremities, head

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: days to weeks, persistent, acute
  Negative indicators: hours, minutes, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: productive cough, chest pain, dyspnea, shortness of breath
  Negative indicators: dry cough, no respiratory symptoms, abdominal pain

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: lying flat, exertion, activity, breathing
  Negative indicators: rest, oxygen, antibiotics

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: antibiotics, oxygen, positioning, rest
  Negative indicators: exertion, lying flat, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, persistent, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 6-8/10, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, severe

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 High fever >102°F

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure

  🚨 Severe chest pain

  🚨 Hemoptysis

  🚨 Signs of sepsis or shock
