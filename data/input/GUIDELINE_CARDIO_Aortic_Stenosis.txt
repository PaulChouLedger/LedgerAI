CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chest pain with exertion
  • shortness of breath
  • syncope
  • dizziness with exertion

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Progressive over months to years, acute decompensation possible. LOCATION: Chest (retrosternal angina), generalized dyspnea. DURATION: Progressive symptoms. CHARACTER: Exertional angina (pressure, squeezing), exertional dyspnea, exertional syncope (classic triad: angina, dyspnea, syncope). AGGRAVATING: EXERTION (angina and syncope), lying flat (dyspnea). RELIEVING: Rest. TIMING: Predictable with exertion. SEVERITY: Variable, 5-8/10 for angina. KEY POSITIVES: Elderly patient, harsh systolic ejection murmur (loudest at right upper sternal border), radiates to carotids, narrow pulse pressure, delayed carotid upstroke, history of bicuspid aortic valve. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT at rest typically.

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: progressive over months to years, acute decompensation possible, gradual
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, retrosternal angina, generalized dyspnea
  Negative indicators: right side only, no radiation, localized point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive symptoms, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: exertional angina, pressure, squeezing, exertional dyspnea, exertional syncope
  Negative indicators: at rest, sharp, stabbing, no symptoms

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, angina and syncope, lying flat, dyspnea
  Negative indicators: rest, medication, position

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: rest
  Negative indicators: exertion, activity, lying flat

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: predictable with exertion, consistent
  Negative indicators: unpredictable, at rest, random

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: variable, 5-8/10, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-4/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Syncope (especially exertional)

  🚨 Severe or new-onset dyspnea

  🚨 Angina (chest pain)

  🚨 Heart failure symptoms

  🚨 Syncope with exertion (high risk)

  🚨 Severe stenosis on echo

  🚨 Low gradient severe aortic stenosis (AS)
