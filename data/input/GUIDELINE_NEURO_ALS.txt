CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • ALS
  • amyotrophic lateral sclerosis
  • muscle weakness
  • muscle atrophy
  • difficulty speaking
  • breathing problems
  • progressive weakness

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over months to years. LOCATION: BILATERAL weakness, usually starting in hands or feet. DURATION: Chronic progressive condition. CHARACTER: PROGRESSIVE weakness, muscle atrophy. AGGRAVATING: ACTIVITY, stress, fatigue. RELIEVING: SUPPORTIVE care, medications. TIMING: Chronic progressive, symptoms worsen over time. SEVERITY: 4-8/10, moderate to severe. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: PROGRESSIVE WEAKNESS, muscle atrophy, difficulty speaking, breathing problems, may have cognitive changes. KEY POSITIVES: Progressive weakness, muscle atrophy, difficulty speaking, breathing problems. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, months to years, slow onset, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate, hours

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral weakness, hands or feet, both sides, generalized
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized, focal

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: chronic progressive, ongoing, persistent, worsening
  Negative indicators: acute, brief, transient, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: progressive weakness, muscle atrophy, moderate to severe, chronic
  Negative indicators: mild, focal deficits, localized, acute

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: activity, stress, fatigue, exertion
  Negative indicators: supportive care, medications, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: supportive care, medications, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: activity, stress, fatigue, exertion

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic progressive, worsening over time, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: moderate to severe, 4-8/10, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-3/10, severe, 9-10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe respiratory distress - possible respiratory failure, call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911
