CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • guillain barre
  • paralysis
  • muscle weakness
  • numbness
  • tingling
  • difficulty walking
  • breathing problems

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: GRADUAL onset over days to weeks. LOCATION: BILATERAL weakness, usually starting in legs. DURATION: Progressive over days to weeks. CHARACTER: PROGRESSIVE weakness, numbness, tingling. AGGRAVATING: MOVEMENT, breathing. RELIEVING: IMMUNOGLOBULIN, plasmapheresis. TIMING: Progressive over days to weeks. SEVERITY: 6-10/10, severe and progressive. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: PROGRESSIVE WEAKNESS, numbness, tingling, difficulty walking, breathing problems, may have facial weakness. KEY POSITIVES: Progressive weakness, numbness, tingling, difficulty walking, breathing problems. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no focal neurological deficits.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, days to weeks, slow onset, progressive
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, immediate, hours

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: bilateral weakness, usually starting in legs, both sides, generalized
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized, focal

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: progressive over days to weeks, ongoing, persistent, worsening
  Negative indicators: acute, brief, transient, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: progressive weakness, numbness, tingling, severe and progressive
  Negative indicators: mild, focal deficits, localized, acute

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: movement, breathing, activity, exertion
  Negative indicators: immunoglobulin, plasmapheresis, rest, treatment

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: immunoglobulin, plasmapheresis, rest, treatment
  Negative indicators: movement, breathing, activity, exertion

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: progressive over days to weeks, worsening, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe and progressive, 6-10/10, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, moderate, variable

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe respiratory distress - possible respiratory failure, call 911

  🚨 Signs of respiratory failure - difficulty breathing, cyanosis, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911
