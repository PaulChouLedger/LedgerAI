## Engineering [2026-06-09]
We cache the FAISS index in memory after first load. Subsequent RAG queries hit the in-memory index — retrieval is sub-10ms. The bottleneck is embedding the query, not searching [follow-up from 2025-12-21 discussion].

## Engineering [2026-05-13]
The RAG chunk size is 200 words with 50-word overlap. Smaller chunks (100 words) gave better precision but worse recall. 200 words is the sweet spot for our embedding model [follow-up from 2026-04-25 discussion].
