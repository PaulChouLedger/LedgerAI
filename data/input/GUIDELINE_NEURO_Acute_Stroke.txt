CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • stroke
  • facial droop
  • arm weakness
  • speech difficulty
  • sudden weakness
  • numbness
  • vision problems

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: SUDDEN onset within minutes to hours. LOCATION: UNILATERAL weakness, numbness, or paralysis. DURATION: Immediate onset, symptoms persist. CHARACTER: SUDDEN, SEVERE weakness, numbness, or paralysis. AGGRAVATING: MOVEMENT, speaking, swallowing. RELIEVING: IMMEDIATE medical intervention, thrombolytics if within window. TIMING: Sudden onset, symptoms persist. SEVERITY: 8-10/10, severe and unrelenting. ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: FACIAL DROOP, arm weakness, speech difficulty, vision problems, dizziness, confusion, headache. KEY POSITIVES: Positive FAST test (Face, Arm, Speech, Time), unilateral weakness, facial droop, speech difficulty. KEY NEGATIVES: No fever, no neck stiffness, no photophobia.

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, minutes to hours, immediate, acute
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: unilateral, one side, face, arm, leg, speech area
  Negative indicators: bilateral, both sides, generalized

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: immediate onset, symptoms persist, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: sudden, severe, weakness, numbness, paralysis
  Negative indicators: mild, gradual, intermittent

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: movement, speaking, swallowing, activity
  Negative indicators: rest, medication, position

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: immediate medical intervention, thrombolytics, emergency treatment
  Negative indicators: rest, home remedies, delayed treatment

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: sudden onset, symptoms persist, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 8-10/10, severe, unrelenting, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, 1-7/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Sudden severe headache with neck stiffness - possible hemorrhagic stroke, call 911

  🚨 Severe neurological deficits with altered mental status - possible large stroke, call 911

  🚨 Signs of increased intracranial pressure - severe headache, vomiting, altered mental status - call 911

  🚨 Severe hypertension with neurological deficits - possible hypertensive crisis, call 911

  🚨 Signs of brainstem involvement - dysphagia, dysarthria, ataxia - call 911
