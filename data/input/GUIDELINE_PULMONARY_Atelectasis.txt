CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • shortness of breath
  • chest pain
  • collapsed lung
  • atelectasis
  • breathing problems after surgery

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual or sudden, often post-operative or bedridden. LOCATION: Focal or segmental lung areas. DURATION: Hours to days, can be persistent. CHARACTER: Mild dyspnea, chest discomfort. AGGRAVATING: Lying flat, shallow breathing. RELIEVING: Deep breathing, coughing, incentive spirometry, positioning. TIMING: Often worse in morning, improves with activity. SEVERITY: Mild to moderate (3-6/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Mild dyspnea, dry cough, chest discomfort, low-grade fever (if infection develops). KEY POSITIVES: Recent surgery, immobility, shallow breathing. KEY NEGATIVES: No severe dyspnea, no chest pain (unless severe), no wheezing.

N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual or sudden, often post-operative or bedridden, variable
  Negative indicators: chronic, persistent, ongoing

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: focal or segmental lung areas, lung, chest
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, specific point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: hours to days, can be persistent, variable
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: mild dyspnea, chest discomfort
  Negative indicators: no severe dyspnea, no chest pain, severe

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: lying flat, shallow breathing, immobility
  Negative indicators: deep breathing, coughing, incentive spirometry

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: deep breathing, coughing, incentive spirometry
  Negative indicators: lying flat, shallow breathing, immobility

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: often worse in morning, improves with activity, variable
  Negative indicators: continuous, persistent, ongoing

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: mild to moderate, 3-6/10, 3, 4, 5, 6
  Negative indicators: severe, 7-10/10, mild, 1-2/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 High fever >101.5°F

  🚨 Chest pain with breathing

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Hemoptysis

  🚨 Signs of infection

  🚨 Progressive respiratory failure

  🚨 Altered mental status
