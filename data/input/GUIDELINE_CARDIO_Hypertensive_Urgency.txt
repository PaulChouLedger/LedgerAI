--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • high blood pressure
  • headache
  • elevated blood pressure

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Gradual, discovered incidentally or with mild symptoms. LOCATION: Headache if present (frontal or occipital). DURATION: Ongoing, chronic. CHARACTER: Asymptomatic or mild headache. AGGRAVATING: Medication non-compliance, stress, salt intake. RELIEVING: Blood pressure reduction. TIMING: Chronic, stable. SEVERITY: Minimal symptoms despite elevated blood pressure (BP). KEY POSITIVES: Elevated blood pressure (BP) (>180/120) WITHOUT end-organ damage, asymptomatic or mild headache only, chronic hypertension history. KEY NEGATIVES: NO chest pain, NO altered mental status, NO vision changes, NO dyspnea (those are hypertensive EMERGENCY).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: gradual, discovered incidentally, mild symptoms
  Negative indicators: sudden, acute, minutes

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: headache if present, frontal, occipital
  Negative indicators: chest pain, vision changes, dyspnea

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: ongoing, chronic, persistent
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: asymptomatic, mild headache
  Negative indicators: chest pain, altered mental status, vision changes, dyspnea

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: medication non-compliance, stress, salt intake
  Negative indicators: blood pressure reduction, medication, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: blood pressure reduction, medication
  Negative indicators: medication non-compliance, stress, salt intake

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: chronic, stable, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: minimal symptoms, despite elevated blood pressure, mild
  Negative indicators: severe, moderate, constant

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe headache (suggests emergency)

  🚨 Vision changes (suggests emergency)

  🚨 Chest pain (suggests emergency)

  🚨 Dyspnea (suggests emergency)

  🚨 Any neurologic symptoms (suggests emergency)

  🚨 Blood pressure >220/120
