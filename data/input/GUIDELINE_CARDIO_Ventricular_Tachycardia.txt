Condition: Ventricular Tachycardia (ventricular tachycardia (VT) / V-Tach)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: emergent
Prevalence: rare
ICD-10 Code: I47.2
SNOMED Code: 25569003
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • palpitations
  • racing heart
  • chest pain
  • lightheadedness
  • syncope

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Sudden, abrupt start. LOCATION: Chest (palpitations), generalized symptoms. DURATION: Sustained (>30 seconds) or non-sustained bursts. CHARACTER: Rapid, regular or slightly irregular palpitations. AGGRAVATING: Exertion, electrolyte abnormalities. RELIEVING: Cardioversion, antiarrhythmics. TIMING: Can be episodic or sustained. SEVERITY: Variable, can be asymptomatic to severe symptoms. KEY POSITIVES: Heart rate 120-250 bpm, wide QRS on electrocardiogram (ECG), history of myocardial infarction (MI) or cardiomyopathy, syncope, chest pain, dyspnea, altered mental status. KEY NEGATIVES: NOT narrow complex tachycardia (SVT).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: sudden, abrupt start
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: chest, palpitations, generalized symptoms
  Negative indicators: localized, one area only, extremities

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: sustained, >30 seconds, non-sustained bursts
  Negative indicators: brief, minutes only, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: rapid, regular, slightly irregular palpitations
  Negative indicators: slow, steady, no palpitations

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: exertion, electrolyte abnormalities
  Negative indicators: cardioversion, antiarrhythmics, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: cardioversion, antiarrhythmics
  Negative indicators: exertion, electrolyte abnormalities, activity

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: episodic, sustained
  Negative indicators: constant, unrelenting, continuous

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: variable, asymptomatic to severe, variable symptoms
  Negative indicators: mild, moderate, constant

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Syncope or loss of consciousness

  🚨 Hemodynamic instability

  🚨 Chest pain

  🚨 Severe dyspnea

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 History of myocardial infarction (MI) or cardiomyopathy

  🚨 History of sudden cardiac death in family

  🚨 Pulselessness (V-Tach with no pulse = ventricular fibrillation )
