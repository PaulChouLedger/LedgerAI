DIAGNOSTIC GUIDELINE: NON-ST-ELEVATION MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION (NSTEMI) (NON-ST ELEVATION MYOCARDIAL INFARCTION)
================================================================================

Condition: non-ST-elevation myocardial infarction (NSTEMI) (Non-ST Elevation Myocardial Infarction)
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: emergent
Prevalence: common
ICD-10 Code: I21.4
SNOMED Code: 401314000
Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • chest pain
  • chest pressure
  • chest discomfort

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Acute, over minutes to hours. LOCATION: RETROSTERNAL or left chest. RADIATES to left arm, jaw, neck, back. DURATION: Prolonged >10-20 minutes. CHARACTER: Pressure, squeezing, heaviness. AGGRAVATING: Can occur at rest or with exertion. RELIEVING: Partial relief with nitroglycerin possible. TIMING: Persistent or crescendo pattern. SEVERITY: 6-9/10. KEY POSITIVES: Troponin elevation (positive cardiac enzymes), dyspnea, diaphoresis, nausea, elderly or diabetic patient, cardiac risk factors. KEY NEGATIVES: NO ST elevation on electrocardiogram (ECG) (differentiates from ST-elevation myocardial infarction (STEMI) but still myocardial infarction (MI)).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: acute, over minutes to hours, sudden
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: retrosternal, left chest, radiates to left arm, radiates to jaw, radiates to neck, radiates to back
  Negative indicators: right side only, no radiation, localized point

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: prolonged, >10-20 minutes, persistent
  Negative indicators: brief, 2-10 minutes, intermittent

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: pressure, squeezing, heaviness
  Negative indicators: sharp, stabbing, burning, crushing

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: can occur at rest, with exertion, variable
  Negative indicators: only with exertion, only at rest, predictable

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: partial relief with nitroglycerin, possible relief
  Negative indicators: complete relief, no relief, predictable relief

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: persistent, crescendo pattern, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: 6-9/10, 6, 7, 8, 9
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-5/10, severe, 10/10

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Elevated troponin (confirms myocardial infarction )

  🚨 Persistent chest pain >20 minutes

  🚨 Diaphoresis

  🚨 Dyspnea

  🚨 Nausea or vomiting

  🚨 Hemodynamic instability

  🚨 Diabetes or chronic kidney disease

  🚨 Elderly patient

  🚨 Multiple cardiac risk factors
