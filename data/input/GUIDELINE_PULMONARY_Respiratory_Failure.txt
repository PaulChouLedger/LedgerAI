Condition: Respiratory Failure
Category: N/A
Urgency Level: emergent

Sex: both

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

CHIEF COMPLAINT TRIGGERS:
This diagnosis should be considered when patient presents with:
  • respiratory failure
  • can't breathe
  • breathing problems
  • respiratory distress
  • shortness of breath

CLASSIC PRESENTATION:
ONSET: Rapid onset over hours. LOCATION: Diffuse, bilateral. DURATION: Acute, persistent. CHARACTER: Severe dyspnea, air hunger. AGGRAVATING: Any exertion. RELIEVING: Mechanical ventilation, oxygen. TIMING: Acute, progressive. SEVERITY: Severe to life-threatening (8-10/10). ASSOCIATED SYMPTOMS: Severe dyspnea, cyanosis, altered mental status, use of accessory muscles. KEY POSITIVES: Severe dyspnea, cyanosis, altered mental status. KEY NEGATIVES: No chest pain (unless secondary), no wheezing (unless reactive airway disease).

TYPICAL PATIENT DEMOGRAPHICS:
N/A

STRUCTURED OLDCARTS ASSESSMENT:
Use this structured data for dynamic questioning:

ONSET:
  Positive indicators: rapid onset over hours, acute, immediate
  Negative indicators: gradual, chronic, slow onset

LOCATION:
  Positive indicators: diffuse, bilateral
  Negative indicators: unilateral, one side only, localized

DURATION:
  Positive indicators: acute, persistent, ongoing
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

CHARACTER:
  Positive indicators: severe dyspnea, air hunger
  Negative indicators: no chest pain, no wheezing, mild

AGGRAVATING:
  Positive indicators: any exertion, activity, movement
  Negative indicators: mechanical ventilation, oxygen, rest

RELIEVING:
  Positive indicators: mechanical ventilation, oxygen, rest
  Negative indicators: any exertion, activity, movement

TIMING:
  Positive indicators: acute, progressive, continuous
  Negative indicators: intermittent, episodic, comes and goes

SEVERITY:
  Positive indicators: severe to life-threatening, 8-10/10, 8, 9, 10
  Negative indicators: mild, 1-7/10, moderate

⚠️  EMERGENCY WARNING SIGNS / RED FLAGS:
If patient reports any of the following, urgent action required:

  🚨 Severe dyspnea or respiratory distress

  🚨 Cyanosis or oxygen saturation <90%

  🚨 Altered mental status

  🚨 Use of accessory muscles

  🚨 Inability to speak

  🚨 Signs of respiratory arrest

  🚨 Severe anxiety or panic

  🚨 Signs of multi-organ failure
